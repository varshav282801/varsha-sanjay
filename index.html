Day One

Saturday 28 November 2026

  1. Engagement

    11:00 – 11:45 am

    Our families exchange thamboolam (betel leaves, nuts and fruit), read out the lagna patrikai (our wedding announcement) and we exchange rings. Followed by lunch.

  2. Maapillai Azhaippu

    5:30 – 6:00 pm · Welcoming the Groom

    Marks Sanjay's joyful arrival, welcomed in with music, dancing and blessings from both families.

  3. Reception & Dinner

    6:00 – 9:00 pm

    On the eve of the wedding, we meet everyone as a couple and seek the blessings of our families and friends.

  4. Sangeet

    9:00 – 10:00 pm

    Come and dance with us, and celebrate the union to come.

Day Two

Sunday 29 November 2026

  1. Kaasi Yatra

    6:00 – 6:30 am · The Journey to Kashi

    A playful, symbolic ritual: Sanjay sets out for Kashi, until Varsha's father persuades him to marry instead.

  2. Oonjal

    6:30 – 7:30 am · The Swing Ceremony

    Family gather round to rock us gently on the swing, blessing us and warding off the evil eye.

  3. Muhurtham

    7:45 – 8:45 am · The Wedding Vows

    Sanjay ties the thaali, the knot that binds us for life.
